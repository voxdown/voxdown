01 The format

Plain text that knows who speaks, and how.

VoxDown is Markdown with a handful of marks. The speaker goes before the quote, the direction goes in brackets, and everything else is simply your story.

  • *never*

    Stress. Leaned on when read aloud, italic in the book.

  • {pause 2s}

    Silence, exactly as long as the moment needs.

  • [door slams]

    A sound in the world of the story.

  • @[sighs]

    The speaker’s own sound, right inside the quote.

  • ::: whispering

    A whole passage under one voice or one direction.

  • %% louder each line %%

    A note for the performer, never read aloud.

  • {book only}

    Print or play. Decide what the book shows and what the audio hears.

  • <!-- fix this -->

    A comment to yourself, never read and never printed.

Still just text. Any editor opens it, Git diffs it, and a Markdown viewer still shows a readable story.

02 One file, every form

Seven lines. Five lives.

Scroll on and watch one moment from the dragon story become everything it needs to be.

  1. Plain

    It starts as words.

    The way every story starts: typed anywhere, in any editor, with nothing to install and nothing to learn yet.

  2. Marked

    Now it knows who’s talking.

    Put a name before a quote and a direction in brackets. The Writer gives each character a colour, so you see your cast at a glance.

  3. Book

    A page ready to print.

    The marks step aside. Directions disappear, stress turns to italics, and what’s left is a clean page for print, Word or EPUB.

  4. Script

    A script to perform.

    Every line with its speaker and its direction, for a table read or a session in the booth. Each performer can take away just their own sides.

  5. Audiobook

    A voice for every character.

    The marks tell the voices who and how. Here are these seven lines as they were recorded, straight from the text.

the-dragon-who-would-not-roar.vxd PlainMarkedBookScriptAudio

And more from the same file: each performer’s sides, lines as CSV or JSON, a standard manuscript to submit, and a production file for audio tools.

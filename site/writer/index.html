          Name the speakers with AI

          The Writer uses the AI and the voices on your own computer, through a small helper. In a terminal, run:

          npx -y @voxdown/cli connect

          It opens the Writer, connected, and keeps running while you work; if this page still says it isn't connected, open the address it prints. It needs Node.js 20 or later, and Chrome, Edge or Firefox, which ask once to let this page reach apps on your computer.

          Your story stays yours. VoxDown has no server and no account: your story goes from this page to the helper on your own computer, and from there only to the AI you choose. Your keys and sign-ins stay on your computer, and VoxDown never sees them. How it works

          VoxDown Writer

          A place to write stories to be read aloud: who says each line, how it is said, the sounds and the pauses, seen as a script and as a book while you write.

          Private by design. No server, no account: your stories stay in this browser and in your own files. Nothing you write is sent anywhere unless you ask for it, and then only to the AI or voice service you choose.

          Free and open source: the code under the MIT license, the format and its documents under CC BY 4.0. Made by Etienne Ackermann.

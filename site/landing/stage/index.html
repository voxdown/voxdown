I

Act I

Write it.

It begins as plain words, because that is what it is. A VoxDown story is a text file: any editor opens it, Git diffs it, and a Markdown viewer still shows a readable story. Nothing is locked inside an app.

  1. Plain text

    Just the story.

    A knight creeps into a cave. A dragon is asleep. Read it in any app you like. So far, it is only words.

  2. Act II

    Mark it.

    Now tell the story how to be performed. A handful of marks, all of them readable by a human.

    @Gerald @Percival

    Who says it.

    An @ and a name before a quote gives it a speaker. Anything without a name is the narrator’s, so prose stays prose.

  3. (deep, polite and utterly unbothered)

    How it’s said.

    A direction in brackets is how the line is played. ::: whispering sets the mood for a whole passage, until ::: lifts it.

  4. [snores] {pause}

    What the room hears.

    Square brackets are sounds, the world’s or a character’s own. Braces hold the silences and the page turns. Notes for the performer are never read aloud; comments for yourself are never read or printed.

    Backstage · VoxDown Writer

    Every tool of the craft, and a cast.

    VoxDown Writer is a free writing app that runs in your browser and knows who is speaking. It gives anyone writing for the ear a novelist’s workshop, and a novelist a cast.

    The scene board

    Chapters as columns, scenes as cards, each with a line and a state. Drag a card and its text moves with it, or press Alt+↑/↓.

    Bring your own AI, if you like

    “Name the speakers with AI…” and “Direct the reading with AI…” work with any AI chat you already use. Nothing is sent until you press a button.

    1. 1Copy the request
    2. 2Paste the answer back

    Marks are only added where your words stay exactly as written. Or connect the AI on your computer with npx -y @voxdown/cli connect.

    Scraps & comments

    Cut a passage to set it aside, bring it back later. Leave yourself comments as you go; they are never read aloud or printed.

    “Most of them,” he said, after a long, biscuit-shaped pause.Too much? Keep for later.

    Built for the long haul

    A 150,000-word book opens in about a quarter of a second, and typing stays at one frame. Every command works from the keyboard (Ctrl+/ lists the shortcuts), with screen-reader labels and a phone layout. Drafts are kept as you type, and named versions wait to be restored.

    The house is yours

    Your story stays on your computer. There’s no VoxDown server to send it to.

    • No account. Open the Writer and write. Files open from your computer and save back there.
    • No tracking. No analytics, no ads. The site is static pages on GitHub.
    • Works offline once it has loaded, and keeps your draft as you type.
    • Open. The code is MIT and the format’s spec is CC BY 4.0. Read it, fork it, build on it.

    For the curious developer

    The format is a spec, not a secret. Parse it, render it, or put the editor in your own page.

    # the parser, the renderer, the editor
npm install voxdown @voxdown/render @voxdown/editor
# the command line: check, convert, connect, tag, produce, mcp
npx -y @voxdown/cli connect
